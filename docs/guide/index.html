Lumae is a music player for the library you already own: your music on Navidrome or Jellyfin, understood through AudioMuse-AI's sound analysis. This guide takes you from a fresh install to your first mix, then through the things people ask about most. Lumae is in beta; What's new lists every build and update.

Before you start

Music server
Navidrome, or Jellyfin 12.0 or newer. Other Subsonic servers, Plex, Emby and Lyrion aren't supported.
Sound analysis
AudioMuse-AI 3.2 or newer, with your library analyzed, and the latest Lumae Analysis from its plugin catalog. Older Lumae Analysis versions work with fewer features; Jellyfin needs 1.6.0 or newer.
Phone
Android 7.0 or newer, or an iPhone on iOS 16.4 or newer, with the beta from Google Play or TestFlight. On iPhone, servers outside your home network need HTTPS.
Optional
Your own AI service, cloud or self-hosted; a Last.fm API key for album suggestions; Koito for your listening history.

No server yet? Demo Mode shows you around with five royalty-free songs.

Set up Lumae

  1. Install Lumae Analysis. In AudioMuse-AI, open Plugins and install Lumae Analysis from the catalog. Turn on its collection manager if you want Collections, shelves, Vibes, journeys and covers to sync between phones.
  2. Start setup. Open Lumae and tap Start setup. On iPhone, allow Local Network access when your phone asks.
  3. Connect AudioMuse-AI. Enter its address, then sign in with Username & password or an API token (leave the token empty if your AudioMuse-AI has no login). Test & connect shows which Lumae Analysis it found and how much of your library is analyzed.
  4. Connect your music server. Choose Navidrome or Jellyfin and sign in. Jellyfin also offers Quick Connect: Lumae shows a code to approve in another Jellyfin app you're signed in to, and keeps only Jellyfin's sign-in for this phone, never your password.
  5. Import listening history from your music server or Koito, if you like. It's optional.
  6. Discovery & AI sets up an AI service and album suggestions. Skip for now leaves them off; both live in Settings later.
  7. Listen. A short guide shows you around while the first import runs. Tap Continue while setup runs to start browsing. On Android, setup keeps going in the background with a “Preparing your music library” notification; on iPhone, it keeps going while Lumae is open.

Lumae checks both servers before it changes anything on your phone. If the first import stops, try again from Library ☰ → Sync status. Settings live in Library's ☰ menu.

Artists showing album covers instead of photos? Add deezer to Navidrome's ND_AGENTS.

Behind a reverse proxy

If AudioMuse-AI sits behind a proxy with its own sign-in (Traefik forward auth, Authelia, Authentik and the like), let these paths through without that sign-in. AudioMuse-AI still checks Lumae's own login on each of them. A blocked path answers with the proxy's sign-in page, and Lumae says “Couldn't ask AudioMuse-AI”.

Keep music offline

  • Keep offline is in the ⋯ menu of an album, playlist or collection, and is a button on an artist's page. For a saved vibe, touch and hold it in Vibe → Saved Vibes.
  • Smart downloads (Settings → Downloads) keeps something to play on the phone within a budget you set: your taste, music you rated four or five stars, what you played recently, and offline vibes. What you keep offline yourself is never removed.
  • Full library mirror, on the same page, keeps whole libraries on the phone, outside the budget, at the mirror quality you choose.
  • Download over Wi-Fi only or any network, and Download quality (Original, or a smaller 192 kbps copy) are in Settings → Downloads. On Android, Storage location can move downloads to an SD card. Settings → Downloads → Manage downloads shows what's on the phone.
  • Buffer ahead (Settings → Playback) downloads the next songs in the queue while you stream, 6 by default and up to 36, so a tunnel or a dead spot doesn't stop the music.
  • Offline mode is the tile at the top of Settings. Auto goes offline when your server is out of reach, On plays downloads only, Off always tries to stream. Offline, lists, search, suggestions and the Vibe tools show only songs on this phone, and plays and ratings wait to reach your server.

Play on speakers

Tap Play on a speaker on Home, or the output button on Now Playing, to open Play on.

  • iPhone: AirPlay (AirPods, HomePod, receivers) and Chromecast.
  • Android: Chromecast, and Sonos, Marantz (HEOS) and UPnP/DLNA speakers on your network: TVs, streamers, receivers, Kodi. Turn on Settings → Car & speakers → Network speakers.

On Android, Settings → Car & speakers → Speakers puts favorites at the top of Play on, hides speakers you never use, and adds one Lumae doesn't find by itself: a Sonos or Marantz by its IP address, a UPnP/DLNA speaker by its description link (such as http://192.168.1.50:1234/description.xml). Under Volume buttons, choose how far each press moves a speaker's volume.

To bring the music back, choose This phone or This iPhone in Play on. While casting, SmoothFade, loudness normalization and the Parametric EQ are off and songs play at their own levels; steering still works.

In the car

Lumae works in Android Auto and CarPlay with nothing to set up. The car shows Home (The Pulse, Instant Play, Comfort mixes, Suggested vibes, what you played and added recently, and Continue journey), Vibes, Library and Downloads. CarPlay's Up Next button and Android Auto's queue show what's coming.

  • Instant Play for the car is in Settings → Car & speakers → Android Auto, or Settings → CarPlay.
  • Resume last queue (Settings → Playback) decides how long Lumae picks up where you left off: off, 30 minutes, 2 hours (the default), 8 hours or always. On Android, Resume from Play buttons lets a car or headset start it with Play.
  • Keep offline what you'll want on the road; Buffer ahead covers the dead spots.

Home-screen widgets

  • Lumae Radio shows what your radio is playing, with Play/Pause, Next and Ban. On Android, add Radio or Radio, small and choose a radio; touch and hold the widget to choose another. On iPhone (iOS 17 or later), add Lumae Radio in small or medium. It needs a radio set up in Settings first.
  • Now playing (Android) shows what Lumae plays, with Play, Next and Love, and follows a Sonos or Marantz speaker while one plays. It needs no setup.

If an Android widget falls behind, the radio remote offers to open App info: set App battery usage to Unrestricted to keep it current.

Sound

Everything here is in Settings → Sound.

  • SmoothFade blends each song's real ending into the next one's opening, using the profiles Lumae Analysis makes (or analysis on the phone). Party mode lets the ending song play on while the next comes in fast.
  • Loudness normalization evens out songs at Quiet, Normal or Loud. Album loudness (Auto, Track or Album) decides whether a whole album keeps its own balance between songs.
  • Ease loud starts, with SmoothFade and loudness normalization both on: when a quiet ending runs into a much louder opening, the next song comes in lower and rises to its level after the fade.
  • Parametric EQ (Android): up to 10 filters per preset, a preset per output route, and import and export of Equalizer APO, AutoEq and Lumae files. Output gain adjusts the level over Bluetooth.
  • Play at 44.1 kHz (Android, under Compatibility): some music players, such as the HiBy R4, let an app play only one audio stream above 44.1 kHz. On those, each song goes silent about 15 seconds before the next one starts. Turn this on if you hear that. Every song is then converted to 44.1 kHz, so hi-res files play at CD quality. It starts with the next song.

Discuss with AI and the AI DJ

Lumae's AI features use your own AI service. Set it up in Settings → Taste & discovery → AI Discovery: OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or self-hosted (Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server). AI-written titles also need Share music details, under Discovery sources.

  • Discuss with AI on Discover: describe a moment and get picks from your library. Switch from Quick picks to AI DJ for a whole set.
  • AI DJ: talk about who's there, how it should feel and what to leave out, then Build the mix. Lock in keeps a song where it is, More like this steers, and Play the set starts it. While it plays, Talk to the DJ on Now Playing or in the queue changes where it goes next.
  • Extend with AI on a playlist or collection adds songs that fit it.

Undertone

Focus, study and sleep music, from your library or one set aside for it. It plays in sessions of its own, off the record, and never mixes into your listening.

  1. Turn it on in Settings → Undertone → Use Undertone, and choose a library on your music server, or another Navidrome or Jellyfin server.
  2. Prepare your music. Folders, tags, even volume, and details such as Effect, Vocals and your own fields, filled in with a spreadsheet. Prepare your music for Undertone walks through it and has the template; the app has the short version under How to prepare your music.
  3. Make presets in categories: what plays, a timer (15 to 90 minutes, or until a time), what happens at the end (fade out or finish the track), and an alarm.
  4. Play. Open Undertone from Library ☰ or its Home button. In a session, add or take off 10 minutes, or Stop and go back to your music.

Alarms need permission: on Android, allow Alarms & reminders, and full-screen alarms to ring on the lock screen (Settings → Undertone → Alarms shows both). On iPhone, alarms need iOS 26 or later; the timer works on any.

Lumae Radio

Lumae Radio plays stations from your library on a Raspberry Pi, and your phone is its remote. Find and adopt it in Settings → Servers & accounts → Lumae Radio. The Lumae Radio guide covers installing the Pi, setup, Home Assistant and the API.

Updates

Install the newest build from TestFlight or Google Play when it appears. Smaller fixes arrive by themselves: open Lumae, leave it open for about 20 seconds, then close and reopen it. Settings → About & help shows the version you run, and What's new says what changed.

Settings travel with your phone's own backup on Android, but sign-ins never do: after a restore, sign in again in Settings → Servers & accounts.

Get help

  • Sync status (Library ☰) shows Server health and Sync health, and can sync everything again.
  • Export diagnostic log (Settings → About & help → Diagnostics) saves a log with names, sign-ins and addresses removed. Email it to apps@rendyjansen.com rather than posting it in a public issue.
  • Report anything else on GitHub, with your phone, your music server, AudioMuse-AI and Lumae Analysis versions, your library's rough size, and what you were doing.
